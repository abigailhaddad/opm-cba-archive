PARTIES TO THE AGREEMENT

This Memorandum of Agreement (“MOA” or the “Agreement”) is entered
into by and between the authorized representatives of the Department of

Control Tower (AEX), Louisiana Air National Guard, Department of the

shall be valid unless made in writing and executed by the Parties.

ARTICLE 9
DISCIPLINARY/ADVERSE ACTIONS

Section 1. For the purposes of this Agreement, informal discipline is defined as oral
admonishments and written warnings. Formal discipline includes letters of
reprimand, suspensions, involuntary reductions in grade or pay, and
removal. An adverse action is defined as a removal, an indefinite
suspension, a suspension of more than 14 calendar days, a reduction-in-
grade or pay, or a furlough for 30 calendar days or less.

Section 2. Actions may not be taken against a BUE under this Article except for such
cause as will promote the efficiency of the service. Actions must be
supported by a preponderance of the evidence. Actions must be
determined on the merits of each individual case.

Section 3. All facts pertaining to any disciplinary or adverse action shall be
developed as promptly as possible. Actions under this Article shall be
promptly initiated after all the facts have been made known to the official
responsible for taking action.

Section 4. If a BUE is the subject of an adverse action falling under the jurisdiction
of the MSPB (such as removal, suspension of more than 14 days,
reduction-in-grade or pay, or furlough of 30 days or less) he or she may
appeal that action to the MSPB or the Union may submit the action to
arbitration under this Agreement, but not both.

Section 5. A BUE against whom action is proposed under this Article, and his or her
Union Representative, shall have the right to review all of the information

Section 3. Notification. The Union agrees to give prompt, written notification to the
Employer in the event an employee participating in the dues deduction
program ceases, for any reason, to be a member in good standing, in order
that the employee’s allotment be terminated. All deductions of dues
provided for in this Article shall be terminated under the following
circumstances immediately upon receipt of appropriate documentation in
the appropriate payroll processing center:

a. in the event of the BUE’s death, retirement, separation from
Federal service, or transfer from Alexandria, LA, AEX ATCT to
another installation, or to a position which is not part of the
bargaining unit; or

b. when notified by the Union, in writing, that the employee has
resigned, been suspended or expelled by the Union.

Section 4. Dues Revocation. In accordance with 5 U.S.C. § 71 15(a), a one-year
period, during which a dues authorization may not be revoked, will
begin from the date on which the employee authorized dues withholding.

Section 5. Dues Amount. The amount of dues withheld under this Article shall be the
regular dues of the member as specified on the member’s SF 1187, or as
certified by the Union if the amount of regular dues has been changed as
provided in Section 6 of this Article. A deduction of regular dues shall be
made each pay period from the pay of an employee who has requested
such allotment of dues. It is understood that no deduction for dues shall be
made in any pay period for which the employee’s net earnings after
deductions is insufficient to cover the full allotment of dues.

Section 6. Changes in Dues. If the amount of regular dues is changed by the Union,
the Union will promptly notify the dues processing payroll center, in
writing, and will certify as to the new amount of regular dues to be
deducted each pay period. The Union will also be responsible for notifying
its members of any changes in the amount of regular dues. New SF 1187
authorization forms will not be required. Changes in the amount of Union
dues for payroll deduction purposes shall not be made more frequently

Remission of Dues. Dues withheld shall be remitted to the Union by check
issued by the appropriate payroll processing center within ten working days
after the officially established payday of each pay period. The check shall be
made payable to NATCA, 1325 Massachusetts Avenue, NW, Washington,
DC 20005. With each check, the Union shall be provided with a list showing
the names of employees, the amount deducted for dues for each employee,
and the amount remitted by the accompanying check. The Union, upon
receiving the listing from DFAS, will verify the members and deductions
and contact the appropriate payroll processing center if any discrepancies
exist. Normally, administrative errors in remittance checks will be corrected
and adjusted in the next remittance check to be issued to the Union.

ARTICLE 11
DURATION

The effective date of the Agreement shall be the date of approval by the
Agency Head or the 31st calendar day after execution of this Agreement,
whichever occurs first. The Agreement shall terminate upon the execution,
ratification, and Agency Head approval of the Parties’ TermCBA.

Nothing in this Agreement binds either Party with respect to negotiating the
provisions of the Parties’ Term CBA.
